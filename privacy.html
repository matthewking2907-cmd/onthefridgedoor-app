Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

The short version. Your family’s plans live on your own devices. If you switch on sync, a copy is kept on our server in London so your devices can share it, and nobody outside your household can see it. We don’t show adverts, we don’t track you around the web, we don’t use analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

Who we are

On the FridgeDoor is a family planner run by Matthew King in the United Kingdom. For anything about your data, email hello@onthefridgedoor.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you put in the app (your family’s names, children’s birth month and year, meal plans, shopping lists, chores, events, tasks, birthdays and gift ideas) is saved in the app on your phone, tablet or computer. Without sync, it never leaves that device.

Some settings always stay on each device and never go to our server: the parent PIN, the colour theme, the kitchen display options and copies of connected calendars.

Only members of your household can read your household’s data. Children don’t have accounts: parents set up the app and decide which devices it’s on.

Children

We ask for each child’s name and birth month and year only, never the full date, so the app can suggest chores and essentials that suit their age. Children don’t sign in, and we don’t collect anything from them directly. A parent can lock the app on a shared device so children can’t change settings or see gift ideas.

Companies that help run the app

We use a small number of services, and only for what’s described here:

  • Supabase runs our database, sign-in and reminder server, on servers in London, UK.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails from Ireland (EU), so it receives the email address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s files. Like any web host, it sees the internet address of devices that load the app.
  • Cloudflare runs our domain’s address book (DNS).
  • Notification services from Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla deliver reminders to your device. They carry the reminder’s text, as with any app notification.
  • Google, Microsoft or Apple, if you connect one of their calendars: our server fetches the calendar from the link you give it.

The app doesn’t load fonts, adverts or trackers from anyone else.

Why we can use your data (UK GDPR)

We use your data to provide the service you’ve asked for: our lawful basis is contract for parents who sign in, and legitimate interests for the family details parents choose to add, which are only used to make the app work for that family.

Your choices and rights

  • See or take a copy: Settings › Backup › Export backup saves everything the app holds for your household as a file.
  • Correct: change anything in the app at any time.
  • Delete: Settings › Sync across devices › More › Delete my account removes your sign-in, your device’s reminders and your calendar links. If you’re the last parent in the household, all of its shared data goes too. Start again in Settings clears a device.
  • Ask us: email hello@onthefridgedoor.com for anything else, including a copy of what we hold.
  • Complain: you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Feedback notes

If you use “Copy feedback note” in Settings, the note contains your device type, app version and which features are on. It never includes names, meals or chores, and it only goes where you paste it.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we’ll update this page and say so in the app before it takes effect.
